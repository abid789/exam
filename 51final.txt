অবশ্যই—**exam-এ লেখার মতো short version**:

## Q1(i): Time Series Plot & Comparative Analysis

**Imports:**  
> The series shows an upward trend with clear quarterly seasonality and irregular fluctuations. Hence, it appears non-stationary.

**GNP:**  
> The series also shows an upward trend and quarterly seasonal pattern. Therefore, it appears non-stationary.

**Comparison:**  
> Both series have similar trend and seasonal behavior, but their fluctuation patterns differ.

---

## Q1(ii): ADF Test

> If **p-value < 0.05**, reject H₀ and conclude that the series is stationary.  
> If **p-value > 0.05**, fail to reject H₀ and conclude that the series is non-stationary.

---

## Q1(iii): STL Decomposition

> STL decomposition separates the series into **trend, seasonal and remainder components**. After removing trend and seasonality, the remainder is approximately stationary if its ADF p-value is less than 0.05.

---

## Q1(iv): Smoothing

> The 4-quarter moving average reduces short-term fluctuations and shows the underlying trend. Holt-Winters smoothing captures **level, trend and seasonal effects** and is suitable for these quarterly series.

---

## Q1(v): ARIMA Model

> An appropriate seasonal ARIMA model is fitted using `auto.arima()`. The model captures the dependence and seasonal pattern and is used to forecast the next 8 quarters.

---

## Q1(vi): Model Diagnostics

> A good model should have residuals randomly distributed around zero with no significant autocorrelation. If the Ljung-Box **p-value > 0.05**, the model is considered adequate.

---

# Q2(i): Unit Root Tests

> ADF and PP have **unit root as H₀**, while KPSS has **stationarity as H₀**.  
> ADF/PP p-value < 0.05 and KPSS p-value > 0.05 indicate stationarity.

---

## Q2(ii): Comparison

> If ADF and PP reject the unit-root hypothesis and KPSS supports stationarity, the series is **stationary**. Thus, all three tests give consistent evidence.

---

## Q2(iii): Remove Unit Root

> If a unit root is present, first differencing is applied to make the series stationary. For the given causal ARMA(2,1) process, the series is theoretically stationary, so **differencing is not required**.
