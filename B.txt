Since the accidental deaths series is monthly and exhibits seasonal variation, a Seasonal ARIMA (SARIMA) model is appropriate for the analysis.
The general form of the model is [SARIMA(p,d,q)(P,D,Q)_{12}].where 12 is the seasonal period because the observations are monthly.
(iv)
summary(model)
checkresiduals(model)
interpret:The MA(1) coefficient of -0.4264 indicates that the previous period's random error has an effect on the current observation. The seasonal MA coefficient of -0.5584 indicates that the previous year's seasonal error also affects the current observation.

The model has (\sigma^2=102999), log-likelihood = -425.53, AIC = 857.06, AICc = 857.50 and BIC = 863.30. The training-set MAPE is 2.35%, indicating a relatively small average percentage error.

The adequacy of the model was checked using the Ljung–Box test. The test gives (Q^*=13.184), with 12 degrees of freedom and a p-value of 0.3558. Since (p>0.05), the null hypothesis of no significant autocorrelation in the residuals is not rejected. Therefore, the residuals are approximately white noise.
